# PB handoff: audit of the current 00–12 pipeline

## Executive summary

The current 00–12 branch starts with **downloaded Maavara monthly GPP and ER reach predictions**, not the later with-area/no-area refits. It routes reach-integrated flux and retained local-catchment area over the filtered CONUS MERIT network, then fits separate ordinary least-squares log–log power laws by HUC8, month, and process. Accumulation includes retained upstream reaches outside the focal HUC8. The saved 07 results contain 22,448 GPP and 22,002 ER eligible fits; median slopes are 1.475 and 1.451, with median \(R^2\) 0.927 and 0.922. These are descriptive fits to modeled, nested cumulative quantities, not independent-site tests or confidence-backed claims of superlinearity.

The strongest consistency evidence is that all 44,450 eligible 07 fits occur in the 08 stability file, and their slopes and \(R^2\) agree to numerical precision. The 08 file also has 1,006 additional groups whose baselines were not eligible for 07. The source CSVs have unique COMIDs per month and no missing rates; 3,300 GPP and 71,968 ER rates are negative and 07 recodes them to NA. The saved 12 segmented summary reports pooled breakpoints near 57 km² (GPP) and 51 km² (ER), but repeated months, nested reaches, and model selection make its AIC improvements descriptive rather than independent evidence of a universal threshold.

Priority interpretation issues are: (1) HUC8 slopes use strongly dependent, overlapping cumulative observations; (2) the 08 deletion range and the 10/11 categorical maps are sensitivity/classification rules, not confidence intervals; (3) route-pair uniqueness, network cycles, and row-level spatial-support alignment have not been independently audited here. Seasonal aggregation has not been chosen: 10/11 require 12 monthly classifications to agree and label disagreements “Seasonally variable.” This is a rule over monthly results, not a seasonal model.

## Scientific and computational definitions

**Response provenance.** `01` reads the downloaded Maavara monthly `scaling_GPP_pred_*.csv` and `scaling_ER_pred_*.csv`. The published final seven-feature forests include upstream watershed area (`uparea`) according to the prior handoff and archived notebooks; 00–12 do not retrain or switch variants. The CSVs and assembled RDS provide one modeled areal rate per COMID per nominal month (12 mean-flow scenarios), not an observed monthly time series. GPP and ER are in g O₂ m⁻² d⁻¹; ER is handled as positive magnitude when positive. Negative model predictions exist and are set to NA in 07, not transformed to their absolute values. No zero rates were found in the 24 raw CSVs.

**Geometry and equations.** For retained reach \(i\), stream surface area is \(S_i=L_i(\mathrm{km})\times1000\times W_i(\mathrm{m})\), in m²; local flux is \(F_{im}=r_{im}S_i\), in g O₂ d⁻¹. The local MERIT catchment area `unitarea` is km². For focal downstream reach \(j\), script 07 uses the same retained upstream lookup \(U(j)\) to calculate \(A_j=\sum_{i\in U(j)}\mathrm{unitarea}_i\) and \(C_{jm}=\sum_{i\in U(j),F_{im}\ \mathrm{available}}F_{im}\). The original MERIT `uparea` is total upstream watershed area and serves as the **reference denominator** in `pct_area_missing = max(0,1−A_j/uparea_j)×100`; it is not the fitted x variable. Process-specific missing metabolism coverage is \((1-\sum_{i:F_{im}\ available}\mathrm{unitarea}_i/A_j)\times100\). The numerator uses local-catchment **area**, not stream surface area or number of reaches. It requires both missing-area and missing-metabolism percentages strictly below 10. A finite positive cumulative area and flux are also required. `sum(..., na.rm=TRUE)` ignores missing fluxes; the separate coverage screen limits their spatial extent. The code checks `pct_metabolism_missing` finite but does not explicitly assert finite `pct_area_missing` in 07's filter; the `<10` comparison drops NA/NaN there.

**Support.** The 05 lookup is built from filtered CONUS MERIT flowlines, with each focal reach included as its own upstream reach. It can cross HUC8 boundaries. HUC8 is the grouping of downstream focal reaches for regression, not a closed accumulation domain. The x and y sums use the same retained lookup, but y omits missing/negative-rate contributions and x includes their catchment area; the coverage screen addresses this asymmetry up to its threshold. A full row-level audit of all 29.3 million route pairs and the spatial joins is still needed before claiming perfect support alignment.

**Power laws.** For each `(huc, month, metabolism_metric)`, 07 fits unweighted OLS \(\log_{10} C_{jm}=\alpha+\beta\log_{10} A_j+\epsilon_j\). It reports intercept, slope, \(R^2\), reach count, area range, and median coverage percentages. It does not save standard errors, confidence intervals, residual diagnostics, or clustered/network-aware inference. Overlapping upstream sets make regression rows dependent. The simple class is low \(R^2\) when \(R^2<0.8\), otherwise slope <0.95 sublinear, 0.95–1.05 near-linear, >1.05 superlinear. The 0.95–1.05 band is a working rule, not an uncertainty interval.

**Stability and seasonality.** 08 deletes confluence-to-boundary sections from regression rows, retaining the original cumulative values. Its min/max slope and \(R^2\) span valid tested deletions only; at most 400 sections are sampled per HUC8, and invalid deletions are excluded. 10/11 require the full fit and all *tested valid* deletions to stay within a slope class, with weakest \(R^2\ge0.8\). `Seasonally variable` means the 12 monthly labels differ, including a mixture of `Uncertain` or `Insufficient data`; it does not imply a statistically estimated seasonal effect. No winter/spring/summer/fall aggregation is performed in 00–12. Flow-scenario comparisons are absent from this production path.

**Segmented fits and figures.** 12 pools all retained reach-month observations **across HUC8s and months**, separately for GPP and ER. The breakpoint is estimated by `segmented` from a median-log-area starting value; there are no explicit breakpoint bounds, minimum numbers on either side, bootstrap intervals, or held-out comparison. It reports OLS and segmented AIC, but no formal adjustment for repeated months or nested network observations. Main panels hexbin the entire 07 input, not a sample. Regional supplements use all 07 rows with a 04c ecoregion assignment, then fit one segment per Level I region and process; missing regions or fit failures are omitted from those panels. Figure 10 maps classes over cached simplified HUC8 polygons, while 11 counts HUC8 classes; neither plots a sample of reaches.

## Findings and targeted follow-ups

### QA-01 — **Potential concern** · interpretation: dependence in cumulative regressions

Evidence: [07 lines 282–347](scripts/07_calculate_huc8_allometric_scaling.Rmd:282) sums shared upstream contributions, then [07 lines 496–525](scripts/07_calculate_huc8_allometric_scaling.Rmd:496) fits unweighted OLS by HUC8-month-process. Adjacent focal reaches share much of their cumulative area and flux. The saved high median \(R^2\) and superlinear slopes describe these constructed nested values; standard OLS uncertainty or an independent-reach interpretation is unsupported. **Observed impact:** dependence is built into the data; its effect on slope uncertainty is unmeasured. **Affected:** the strength of power-law and superlinearity claims, 07 plots, 10/11 classifications, 12 segmented comparison. **Query to Codex:** “For a small representative set of HUC8-month fits, how much do slopes and classifications change under a predeclared, network-aware thinning or section-level resampling diagnostic?” **Resolution:** paired diagnostic with explicit sampling unit and uncertainty definition, without silently replacing the current descriptive fit.

### QA-02 — **Potential concern** · interpretation: 10/11 category names can be read inferentially

Evidence: [08 lines 20–30](scripts/08_huc8_fit_stability.R:20) caps sections at 400; [08 lines 139–212](scripts/08_huc8_fit_stability.R:139) excludes invalid deletions; [10 lines 50–70](scripts/10_figure1_scaling_maps.R:50) and [11 lines 49–69](scripts/11_ecoregion_scaling_bars.R:49) classify using extrema, a working 0.95–1.05 band, and \(R^2\) cutoff. Code comments correctly say these are not CIs, but map labels such as “Linear” and “Uncertain” can be read as inferential conclusions. **Observed impact:** figures implement this deterministic rule; no statistical coverage or type-I error is established. **Affected:** Figure 1, criteria table, Figure 2 narrative. **Query:** “Can you tabulate each final category’s contribution from low \(R^2\), slope-band crossing, missing months, and mixed monthly labels?” **Resolution:** a saved classification ledger and a caption using descriptive language.

## Handoff summary

**Established.** The chain uses downloaded monthly modeled rates, same retained-network routing for cumulative local-catchment area and process-specific cumulative flux, HUC8/month/process unweighted log–log OLS, and a separate section-deletion classification. Output counts, raw negative counts, 07/08 baseline agreement, and 12 summary input counts are directly checked. Median saved slopes and \(R^2\) indicate strong descriptive power-law fits in eligible groups.

**Uncertain.** Full route closure integrity, intermediate filter attrition, exact spatial key mismatches, external-layer/cache versions, original commands/package state, effective independent sample size, and breakpoint uncertainty. Existing artifacts do not support claims of statistically established superlinearity, a universal national breakpoint, or a decided seasonal aggregation.

**Peter’s decisions.** Whether 00–12 should remain a descriptive analysis of published modeled rates; how to interpret or alter the negative-rate and coverage conventions; what uncertainty or network-dependence standard should underlie sub/near/superlinear labels; whether seasons should be aggregated and by what rule. Flow scenarios can remain parked. Existing ancillary analyses should be retained as separate evidence rather than forced into these two questions.

**Three highest-priority targeted checks.** (1) Verify 05 graph/closure integrity because every cumulative value depends on it. (2) Produce a 03→07 key/attrition ledger, including the 117 COMID discrepancy and negative/coverage screens, because spatial support can change fitted samples. (3) Quantify dependence and side support for representative 07/12 fits before making inferential slope or breakpoint claims.
