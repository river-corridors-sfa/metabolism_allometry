# ER training-label provenance and prediction-shift diagnostic

Status: **CONFIRMED descriptive findings; scientific response UNRESOLVED** (2026-10-30). Code: `scripts/validation/step1_er_label_and_shift_diagnostic.py`. Read-only analysis of the archived Dryad training CSV and saved original/paired-refit prediction CSVs; no model or production pipeline was rerun.

## What the archived code does

The archived `random_forest_CONUS_ER.ipynb` reads `appling_data_06202024.csv`, multiplies `ER`, `ER.lower`, and `ER.upper` by −1, then trains on all rows. It does not screen the response by sign, interval, `ER.Rhat`, or `ER.n_eff`. The local `01a_refit_maavara_with_without_uparea.py` reproduces that unconditional response sign flip, shared 70/30 split, seed 24, and random-forest configuration for both predictor sets. Its with-uparea production predictions closely reproduce downloaded original predictions (`data/processed/17_maavara_refits/replication_against_downloaded.csv`; monthly ER R² relative to downloaded predictions 0.9969–0.9984).

The training file contains 490,358 ER rows. Under the original StreamMetabolizer sign convention, 43,010 (8.77%) are **positive**; after the flip, those become negative training targets for a model intended to predict positive respiration magnitude. There is no explicit binary quality flag in this CSV. Of the 43,010 positive raw ER estimates, 34,112 exceed +0.5 g O₂ m⁻² d⁻¹, 25,596 have `ER.lower > 0`, and 17,414 have intervals crossing zero. The positive raw ER estimates have median +1.908; 28,342 exceed +1. Both sign groups have median `ER.Rhat` near 1.0003 and median `ER.n_eff` of 2,000, so these columns do not separate the positive raw ER rows. They occur at 318 of 355 sites, including 16 sites with at least half their rows positive. Their frequency is 13.5% of January and 13.2% of February training rows, versus 4.1–5.6% in July–September. See `training_label_quality.csv`, `training_wrong_sign_by_month.csv`, and `training_wrong_sign_by_site.csv`. These fields characterize estimates; they do not independently certify biological validity.

The [Appling et al. source data paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6289110/) calls ER above +0.5 biologically unrealistic and explains that positive ER can reflect oxygen-sensor calibration problems. Its quality assessment also uses model-level diagnostics, whereas `ER.Rhat` and `ER.n_eff` here are per-parameter columns. The Maavara training CSV and notebook do not carry through the source paper's model-level confidence classification as an explicit training-row exclusion.

## Which median shifted?

Across **all** 2,101,392 network reach-months, the ER median is +3.613 in the downloaded original predictions, +3.622 in the with-uparea refit, and +3.748 in the without-uparea refit. The overall median therefore shifts slightly **up**, by +0.126 relative to the paired with-uparea refit. The mean rises from +4.863 to +5.730, and the standard deviation from 4.302 to 6.507. The 5th percentile falls from +0.600 to −0.780 while the 95th rises from +13.483 to +20.259. Removing `uparea` broadens the modeled distribution in both tails; it does not shift the entire distribution negative. `prediction_distribution_by_month.csv` shows the seasonal medians.

The previously discussed +2.35 to −1.25 median difference refers only to the **121,969 selected COMID-months** whose paired refit predictions change from nonnegative with `uparea` to negative without it. In that subset, the with-uparea median is +2.346 and without-uparea median is −1.254 (median paired change −3.812). Another 40,715 rows change in the opposite sign direction, and 30,548 are negative in both refits. The with-uparea model assigned `uparea` 0.170 of impurity-based feature importance, its second-highest value after canopy cover; the no-uparea model reallocates importance among the remaining features. Importance is descriptive, not a causal decomposition.

The newly negative-only group tends to have small upstream area (monthly medians 75–91 km²), low monthly discharge (monthly medians 0.07–0.58 m³ s⁻¹), and low canopy cover (monthly median canopy values around 3–4%). `prediction_sign_groups_by_month.csv` gives exact monthly counts and predictor medians. This is an association with where the two fitted functions disagree, not proof of which feature or training rows drive any individual prediction.

## Interpretation and next decision

The sign-flipped, wrong-sign training targets make negative random-forest outputs possible. The controlled with/without-uparea refits show that omitting `uparea` is associated with a large increase in such outputs. The source labels also show a winter pattern that resembles the negative-prediction seasonality. These observations are compatible with an interaction between response quality, season, and predictor choice, but do not establish a causal mechanism. The archived data do not include an explicit pass/fail flag or a documented rule for discarding positive raw ER in the notebook.

Before changing the production model, the research team should decide how to treat biologically wrong-sign raw ER estimates, including whether near-zero values should be handled differently from clearly positive values and whether intervals or other source diagnostics are sufficient. Any candidate change needs a newly fit paired with/without-uparea comparison on identical train/test records, held-out skill, sign frequencies, screened support, and downstream allometry/classification sensitivity. No such model change or expensive rerun was made here.
