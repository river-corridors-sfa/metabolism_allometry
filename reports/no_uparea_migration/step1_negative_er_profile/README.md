# Negative ER predictions: saved-output profile

Status: **CONFIRMED descriptive profile; scientific interpretation UNRESOLVED** (2026-10-30). Reproducible code: `scripts/validation/step1_negative_er_profile.R`. This reads the two saved 01 assemblies and the saved 03 catchment network. It does not rerun models or alter analysis outputs. Rates are ER respiration magnitudes in g O₂ m⁻² d⁻¹, so negative values are wrong-sign predictions under the current convention.

Across 2,101,392 matched COMID-months, the original downloaded predictions contain 71,968 negative ER rates (3.42%); the no-uparea predictions contain 152,517 (7.26%). Sign transitions are: 30,498 negative in both, 122,019 nonnegative original → negative no-uparea, 41,470 negative original → nonnegative no-uparea, 1,907,345 nonnegative in both, and 60 newly missing. Exact monthly counts are in `sign_transitions_by_month.csv`.

The **controlled refit comparison** is more informative about removing `uparea`: the saved with-uparea refit has 71,263 negative ER predictions (3.39%), versus 152,517 (7.26%) in the paired without-uparea refit. There are 121,969 with-refit-nonnegative → without-refit-negative transitions, offset by 40,715 in the reverse direction; 30,548 are negative in both. Both refits use the same training source, response, and split according to `01a_refit_maavara_with_without_uparea.py`. Monthly counts are in `paired_refit_signs_by_month.csv`. This supports the interpretation that excluding `uparea` in this refit is associated with the large increase in wrong-sign ER predictions; it does not reveal the model mechanism or whether the resulting values should be repaired.

**Training-label finding:** `appling_data_06202024.csv` contains 490,358 ER training rows: 447,348 raw negative ER values and **43,010 raw positive ER values (8.77%)**. The refit code multiplies all raw ER responses by −1 and does not exclude wrong-sign response rows, so the resulting positive-magnitude training target contains 43,010 negative values. This makes negative random-forest predictions mathematically possible. It does not by itself explain why removing `uparea` more than doubles their frequency; it does identify an upstream response-quality decision for scientific review. Counts are in `training_er_signs.csv`.

The no-uparea negative rates have median **−1.352**, 10th percentile **−4.215**, 90th percentile **−0.226**, mean **−1.866**, and minimum **−18.914**. In magnitude, 94,265/152,517 (61.8%) are at least 1, and 48,537/152,517 (31.8%) at least 2. Among the 122,019 newly negative rows, the original rate had median **+2.353** and the new rate had median **−1.254**; these sign disagreements are not confined to values near zero. Complete quantiles and bins are saved separately.

Seasonality is strong: no-uparea negative ER rates occur in 10.9% of source reaches in January–February, versus 3.9–4.0% in July–September. In the filtered 03 catchment network, 29,114 of 164,073 reaches are negative in at least one month; 11,126 are negative in six or more months, and 3,049 in all 12 months. Negative rates occur across all 18 HUC2 regions represented in the network. HUC2 rates range from about 2.0% (04) to 10.2% (15) of reach-months; counts and denominators are in `negative_by_huc2.csv`. These are descriptive spatial groups, not causal explanations.

The step-1 screen audit found 93,884 original-only ER screened observations with a newly negative **focal** prediction. More losses can arise from negative predictions on upstream reaches; downstream observations overlap, so this number must not be added to the screen-rule counts. Negative rates are converted to missing by script 07 before accumulating flux. The resulting no-uparea screen has 72,181 original-only ER rows newly failing positive cumulative flux and 159,910 newly failing metabolism-area coverage. Those are first-failing-rule assignments, not counts of unique negative source predictions.

The profile does not establish why the models disagree or distinguish covariate effects, low-rate uncertainty, and other mechanisms. A next diagnostic should inspect the provenance and quality flags of the 43,010 wrong-sign training responses, then assess held-out performance and prediction signs by month and region before choosing an imputation or model change. No training labels were removed here.
